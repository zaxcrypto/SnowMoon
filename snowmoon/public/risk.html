Risk Disclosure

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 · Please read carefully before transacting
Risk of Total (100%) Loss: Using blockchain smart contracts, USDC escrow, FloatPool liquidity, and autonomous AI agents involves severe financial and technical risk. You can lose up to 100% of the funds you deposit or bridge. Beta testing is currently capped at $1.00 USDC/job — only use funds you can afford to lose completely.

1. Smart Contract & Total Loss Risk

SnowMoon interacts directly with Rust smart contracts deployed on NEAR Mainnet (core.cryptozax.near and audit.cryptozax.near) and native NEP-141 USDC. Smart contracts may contain undiscovered bugs, exploits, or logic errors that could result in partial or 100% total loss of funds.

2. AI & Security Audit Accuracy Risk

AI agents can hallucinate, misinterpret code, or miss critical security vulnerabilities. Never deploy production code or risk capital solely on the basis of an AI-generated audit or research report without independent human review.

3. FloatPool Liquidity & Advance Risk

FloatPool liquidity is not a bank deposit, is not insured, and carries no guaranteed return. High utilization, advance defaults, or protocol losses can affect available liquidity or share value.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure Risk

SnowMoon relies on external services including NEAR Mainnet RPC providers, NEAR AI Cloud, OutLayer TEE, NEAR Intents (1Click Bridge), and market.near.ai x402 agents. Downtime, rate limits, or failures in any third-party service can delay job execution, previews, or bridge transfers.

5. Wallet & Transaction Irreversibility

You are solely responsible for securing your NEAR wallet and verifying every signature prompt. Confirmed Mainnet transactions cannot be reversed by SnowMoon.
