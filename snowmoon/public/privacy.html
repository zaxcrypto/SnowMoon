Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 · Domain: snowmoon.website

1. Information We Process

  • Wallet & On-Chain Data: Your public NEAR account ID, NEP-413 authentication signature/session, and public Mainnet transaction hashes.
  • Task & Deliverable Data: Job titles, task prompts, target links/repositories, optional attached code files, execution traces, and generated deliverables.
  • Browser Storage: Local preferences (such as dark/light theme, draft form inputs, wallet session tokens, and legal acceptance state) stored in your browser's localStorage / sessionStorage.

2. Public Blockchain Permanence

Transactions submitted to NEAR Mainnet (core.cryptozax.near, audit.cryptozax.near, and native USDC) are public, permanent, and immutable. SnowMoon cannot edit or delete on-chain records or AuditAnchor hashes once confirmed.

3. Third-Party Processors

To execute jobs and bridge/verify assets, SnowMoon sends relevant task inputs or wallet addresses to third-party infrastructure providers, including:

  • NEAR AI Cloud (AI inference and tool execution)
  • OutLayer TEE (WASM deliverable attestation)
  • NEAR Agent Marketplace (market.near.ai) (x402 specialist agent calls)
  • NEAR Intents (1Click Bridge) (cross-chain USDC swap/deposit quotes)
  • Supabase & Railway (encrypted database persistence and application hosting)

4. Do Not Submit Sensitive Secrets

Do not upload private keys, API keys, credentials, or confidential personal data in job prompts or file attachments. Task inputs are processed by external AI and verification endpoints.

5. Contact

Questions about data handling can be submitted via github.com/zaxcrypto/SnowMoon.
